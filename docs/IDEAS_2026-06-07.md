## TIER 1 — measure & win the distribution shift (highest value, mostly CHEAP/CPU)
1. **Leave-one-BENCHMARK-out (LOBO) CV.** Re-score the whole canon + stack by holding out an entire
   benchmark (16 of them) instead of random items. This is the honest proxy for the real test. Expect
   the flexible tree-meta to fall and simple non-neg blends to rise. **Re-rank everything under LOBO** —
   our current 0.419-based decisions may not hold. CPU-only, reuses existing OOF infra (regroup by
   `benchmark`), ~fast. DO THIS FIRST — it recontextualizes every other idea.
2. **Calibration layer**, tuned on LOBO: (a) temperature scaling on the stack logit, (b) base-rate
   shrinkage toward the per-benchmark mean pass-rate, (c) isotonic per-benchmark. Likely the single
   biggest real-test gain (the LB gap smells like miscalibration under shift). CPU, minutes.
3. **Shrinkage / fewer-param stacker.** Compare the 24-col non-neg stack vs a 9-col canon-only blend
   vs ridge-shrunk weights vs equal-weight, all judged under LOBO. Fewer params → better transfer.

## TIER 2 — cheap member upgrades from the "bagging wins, params hurt" lesson (GPU, modest)
4. **Bag the other canon members** the way irt_bag beat irt: bagged-etbig (more seeds/row-bootstraps),
   bagged-mlp / snapshot-ensemble mlp (cyclic-LR snapshots averaged). Bagging helps high-variance
   learners (we measured dropout helping mlp +0.029 but bagged trees ~0) → expect mlp to gain most.

6. **Cross-family embedding member**: concatenate/whiten the 3 families' item embeddings into one
